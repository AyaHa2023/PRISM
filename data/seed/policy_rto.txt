PARTNER COMMITTEE MEMO — Consulting Practice
Subject: Mandatory Return-to-Office for Client-Facing Teams

Effective next month, all client-facing consultants (Assurance, Consulting, Tax)
must be in the office 4 days per week (Monday–Thursday). Remote work limited to Fridays.

Rationale:
- Client proximity for audit fieldwork and steering committees
- Onboarding quality for new associates on live engagements
- Partner visibility on utilization and engagement health

Expected ripple effects (workshop notes):
- Associates with long commutes may push back; retention risk in competitive markets
- Engagement Leads worry about Fortune 500 client visit scheduling and travel costs
- Managers anticipate utilization dips during transition weeks
- Shared Services / IT Vendor may see increased laptop and access requests

Stakeholders: Partners, Managers, Senior Consultants, Associates, Engagement Leads,
Fortune 500 Clients, IT Vendor Partners

Metrics to watch: attrition risk, morale, utilization, client SLA, partner overhead
