CONFIDENTIAL — Partner Committee Draft
Subject: Salary & Bonus Adjustment — Consulting Bands 1–3

Due to margin pressure on fixed-fee engagements, leadership is considering a 10%
adjustment to base salary for Band 1–3 consultants, partially offset by a one-time bonus.

Context:
- Multiple Fortune 500 clients renegotiating fees
- Partner committee debating retention vs profitability tradeoff
- Engagement teams already running above target utilization

Likely ripple effects:
- High performers may exit to competitors (Deloitte, EY, boutique firms)
- Managers face difficult retention conversations before year-end reviews
- Clients may perceive instability if key engagement leads turnover mid-audit
- Vendor partners unchanged but shared-services scope may shrink

Stakeholders: Partners, Managers, Consultants, Associates, HR, Key Clients

Metrics: retention risk, morale, utilization, engagement continuity, client trust
